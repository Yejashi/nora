## Problem and evidence

Exact failing test, profile or capability experiment. Include pinned versions/revisions and environment. Separate observations from inference.

## Options

Two or three relevant options with implementation and maintenance cost. Do not include a strawman merely to justify the chosen option.

## Decision

Smallest change that addresses the problem. State changed boundaries and resource/compatibility consequences.

## Validation and follow-up

Acceptance proving the change, tests actually run, remaining hardware checks, docs/cards affected and upgrade/reconciliation responsibility.
